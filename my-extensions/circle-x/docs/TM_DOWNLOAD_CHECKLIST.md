# TM download checklist — Stryker and JLTV

Use this checklist to collect the operator technical manuals needed to add
vehicle-specific PMCS checklists to Circle X. Start with the core manuals, then
collect the specialty and optional manuals for the vehicles you want supported.
One manual can cover several variants; those variants do not each need a separate
download.

Confirm the current edition and all applicable changes when downloading.

## Where to obtain them

The TM links above open official **APD publication records**, rather than the
full manuals. Search by the exact TM number, or the EM number where supplied:

1. **[AESIP](https://login.aesip.army.mil/portal/faces/home)** — the download
   portal identified by APD.
2. **[LDAC ETMs Online](https://ent.ldac.army.mil/etmapp/#/etm/home)** — another
   official route for electronic manuals. The Army's
   [IADS getting-started instructions](https://iads.redstone.army.mil/gettingstarted.html)
   describe NIPR and account access requirements.

## What to collect with each manual

Obtain the **complete operator PDF set or IETM package**, including:

- Title pages, publication date, revision/change number, and all applicable
  change records or change packages.
- All PMCS work packages for before, during, and after operation.
- Associated warnings, cautions, notes, tables, and referenced figures.
- Model applicability, serial-number restrictions, and **Usable On Code (UOC)**
  definitions.
- Original work-package and item numbers so app checks can cite their sources.

For an IETM, preserve the complete package, including its XML or `.iads` files
and associated assets. If exporting PDFs from IADS, include the PMCS work
packages and their referenced material. The package format will determine the
parsing approach.

These are **operator manuals for PMCS**. Maintenance/parts manuals such as
`-20`, `-23&P`, and hand receipts (`-HR`) are not substitutes. Additional
equipment-specific manuals may be needed where the operator TM references them.
